How a night goes

You never stare at a blank page.

Open the app and the question is already there. Recall and record three good things. Then put your phone down.

0:00

Hina asks first.

The first thing on screen is a question, not an empty box. Your companion asks for one good thing, then the next.

0:40

Answer in a sentence.

Coffee that was exactly right. The cat on the warm laundry. Your sister calling for no reason. Record three small moments to complete your reflection.

1:30

Watch it grow.

Every day you write, your plant grows closer to blooming, right in front of you. Hina says so. Then you're done until tomorrow.

Afterward there's one optional tap: how do you feel? Five faces and a Skip button. Your companion answers either way.

For the days you'd rather not write

There's a button for hard days.

Tap “Today was hard” and Hina closes her eyes and sits with you. No entry needed. Nothing is taken away.

  • Your plant never wilts, never dies, never resets.
  • A day you skipped is a soft gray dot on the calendar. Not red, not an X.
  • There is no streak counter anywhere in the app. Four entries, whenever they happen, make a plant bloom.

Private by design

Your journal is saved on your iPhone.

No account registration is needed. Optional AI conversations and weekly letters send text to online AI only with your consent. Photos and mood scores are not sent to AI.

Read the privacy policy

  • No account, no sign-upOpen the app and start writing. That's the whole onboarding.
  • Journaling works offlineWriting, photos, plant growth, and small care work offline. Online AI and App Store purchases need an internet connection.
  • No ads or behavioral trackingAI text and replies are not stored on Mitsuba’s server. Anonymous authentication identifiers and usage counts help prevent misuse.
  • Export or delete any timeSave everything as plain text or JSON, or delete it all in two taps.

Questions

Small print, in plain words.

Is it free?

Yes. Mitsuba is free to download, with no ads. Three rare plants are available as an optional one-time purchase.

What happens if I miss a day?

Nothing. The plant never goes backwards and never dies. The next day you write, it grows one more step.

Do I have to write three things?

Yes. Record three good things to complete your reflection. Your progress is saved if you need to continue later.

Where is what I write stored?

Journal entries and photos are saved on your iPhone. With your consent, AI features send conversation or relevant journal text outside your device to create replies and letters. Photos and mood scores are not sent. You can turn AI off in Settings.

Can I move my entries to a new phone?

Not automatically in this version. Use Settings, then “Export as JSON” to keep a copy.

Which languages does it support?

English, 日本語, 한국어 and 繁體中文. It follows your iPhone's language, and you can change it in Settings.

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

Does small care grow my plant?

Small care has its own garden path. It is optional and does not change your journal progress or plant growth.
